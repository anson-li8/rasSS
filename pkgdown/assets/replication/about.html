About

ras-ss is a summary-statistic extension of the Regional Association Score (RAS) method of Jiang & Zhang (2025). RAS finds trait-associated regions by turning a chromosome’s regional association scores into a time series and running change-point detection (CPD) on it, giving it more power than single-SNP and set-based tests in challenging cases (i.e. sparse causal variants) while keeping the false-positive rate down. The fundamental limitation is that RAS, as published, needs individual-level genotypes and phenotypes, which most GWAS don’t share. ras-ss switches RAS’s per-window regression for a one-degree-of-freedom weighted burden statistic built from marginal Z-scores and an external LD reference, so the same workflow, including the original, unmodified change-point detection, runs on summary statistics alone.

The work so far is a simulation validation published as a reproducible on this site, spanning two scales. On a 300-SNP toy chromosome (see Validation), ras-ss matches the individual-level method on identical data to within one replicate out of 200, maintains type I error within the original paper’s accepted range, and falls behind the 5-averaged original by about six power points due to the missing resampling average. On a 1,000-SNP chromosome replicating the paper’s full simulation design (see Paper Replication), ras-ss controls type I error at exactly 0.05 for both traits (500 null reps, 95% CIs ≈ 0.03–0.08), is close to the individual-level paired method on power across all scenarios, calls ~0 false regions, and generates its association profile ~10× faster than the paired individual-level scan (0.045 vs 0.48 s/rep, closed-form matrix algebra vs per-window regressions), though total per-rep time is similar because both share the same change-point-detection step. A real summary-statistic run is the next step and is not yet done; this site will be updated when it is.

This is my project in Dr. Han’s lab. The method it extends is Jiang & Zhang, Empowering genome-wide association studies via a visualizable test based on the regional association score, PNAS 122(9) e2419721122 (2025); the RAS package is at github.com/hepingzhangyale/RAS.
